The people running a business or a political party are simply resource managers. The works are:

- Pool the money.
- Get or hire people that gets things done.
- Allocate money for operations.
- Allocate money for people.
- Allocate money for marketing.
- Allocate money for sales.

Core activities:

- Defend aganist competition.
- Attack or neutralise possible threats.
- Always try to differentiate.
- Use power to stay in power.

Every system tries to differentiate themselves but mostly work on the same lines.
