I am part of Maarifaa, an ecosystem designed to share knowledge between Indian and Africa. It is a huge idea to execute. Maarifaa is trying to empower African students via Indian education and its ecosystem. 

It is not an easy task. Yet the founder Mr. Venkat has a deep passion for the youth in Africa and wanted to make Indian education accessible to them.

It took almost three years to ideate and crack the ground to setup the project.

Time will tell where Maarifaa can go. If you are interested, you can checkout details at [Official Maarifaa website](https://maarifaa.com)
