I wrote my journey from 104 kgs to 90kgs in Jan 2025. I gained weight and I am now at 98. When I look back why that happened I noticed the following patterns.

##### Improper sleep schedules

For various reasons my sleep schedules got out of sync. Every day I wake up at different time. I then prepared my day from there. It is already late and I am hungry. I could not wait for 30-40min for workout. That delay will irritate my stomach. So I ate immediately. Then followed the work. 

##### Unable to workout in the evenings

I then started to workout in the evenings. It worked for few days then many things went in the way. Work. Chores. Meetings. They outnumbered. I could not keep up the evening rhythm.

##### Family time

When my family was together, the food times and dishes go in a different direction. There is peer pressure and there are different timings to eay. I failed to revolt and keep the consistency.

##### Uncertainity

I had a lot of uncertainity along the days with personal things. I could not keep up with all of them. No two days go the same way. Each day was different.

##### The natural demotivation

The above would come in combinations also. Because of irregular rhythms I could not maintain what is required.
