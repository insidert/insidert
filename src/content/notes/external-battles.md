We do external battles to unify our internal cracks. Be it on personal level or country level. Whenever there are internal conflicts, the people in power needs a way to control it or divert it. Then we do external battles. 

Without external battles, a common unifying theme or a unifying idea is hard to achieve in a short span.

Battles carry an emotional shortcut. It is them or us. We always choose us. So we keep the differences away and become a single voice, a single movement.

Deep below is the delusion. It is hard to understand. Hard to interpret. Hard to find. Hard to teach. Hard to explain.
